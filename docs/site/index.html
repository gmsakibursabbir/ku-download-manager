How it works

Everything a download
manager should do

And a few things none of the others do.

  1. Full speed, automatically

    Every file is split across many connections, and KuDownloader tunes them while it runs: more when the server keeps up, fewer when it pushes back.

  2. Resume after anything

    Lost Wi‑Fi, a restart, a link that expired: pick up where it stopped. Refresh an expired address without losing a byte.

  3. Videos and music from 1,000+ sites

    Choose the quality, keep subtitles and cover art, grab whole playlists, or save just the audio as M4A, MP3, Opus or FLAC.

  4. Takes over the browser

    Downloads you start in the browser land in KuDownloader, with the site's cookies so signed-in downloads work too.

  5. Yours to shape

    Light and dark themes, accent colours, twelve languages, speed profiles, and schedules that start the night shift for you.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. KuDownloader is free and open source, with no ads, no accounts and no paid tier.

Does it collect any data?

No. Your downloads, history and settings stay on your devices. See the privacy policy.

Windows or macOS says the app is from an unknown developer.

The installers are not yet signed with a paid code-signing certificate. The app updates themselves are signed and verified before they install.

Why isn't the Android app on Google Play?

Google Play does not allow apps that download from video sites, so KuDownloader for Android is published here and on GitHub.

Does it download DRM-protected videos?

No. KuDownloader does not bypass DRM.
